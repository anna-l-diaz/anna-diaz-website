Global agricultural biosecurity: an underexamined strategic vulnerability?

An independent investigation of international systems for detecting and responding to crop pests and pathogens, and the potential consequences for economic resilience.

Research outline — not a finished article or established conclusion. This page deliberately does not claim that the issue is underprioritized.

Research question

Are there consequential gaps in U.S. support for international agricultural biosecurity that remain insufficiently addressed despite existing agricultural, food-security, and biological-defense programs?

Areas of investigation

  • How crop pathogens and pests are detected, reported, and contained across borders.
  • Which institutions and programs support agricultural surveillance and response.
  • Where surveillance, incentives, diagnostic capabilities, or implementation may fall short.
  • What evidence would challenge the claim that these risks are insufficiently addressed.

Current status

The research and evidence map are in development. Findings, primary-source citations, competing interpretations, and conclusions should be added only after verification.
