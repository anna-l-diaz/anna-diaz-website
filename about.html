About / Anna Diaz

Curiosity with a practical foundation.

I care about the systems that allow ambitious ideas to become real industrial capability.

Why industry matters to me

I'm an industrial operations professional working in aerospace and defense manufacturing, with a background in systems and operations management.

My interests converge around a question: how can the United States build, produce, and scale more of the technologies and capabilities that underpin its economic vitality and security?

For me, industrial renewal is not only about building factories. It's also about developing a skilled workforce, resilient supply chains, productive organizations, and better technology for the people doing the work.

I want to contribute through operational practice, hands-on technical learning, independent research, and eventually entrepreneurship and advisory work.

This site contains personal, independent work; it does not represent my employer or any organization with which I may volunteer.
