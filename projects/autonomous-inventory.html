Independent engineering exploration / In progress

Autonomous warehouse inventory drones.

Investigating whether autonomous aerial systems could improve inventory visibility and reduce repetitive manual counting in warehouse environments.

Early-stage research only. No prototype, flight validation, or commercial performance claims are implied.

Problem space

Inventory verification is often labor-intensive and dependent on accurate physical location data. Autonomous collection could be useful, but feasibility depends on navigation, sensing, safety, system integration, and the economics of the use case.

Questions I'm investigating

  • What types of warehouses are suitable for aerial inventory collection?
  • How can an indoor vehicle localize without GPS and safely avoid obstacles?
  • What sensing methods reliably identify labels, barcodes, or inventory positions?
  • How do data accuracy, cycle time, and capital costs compare with existing methods?

Next steps

Continue technical literature review, define an independent test environment, and identify the most consequential design tradeoffs before selecting a prototype architecture.
